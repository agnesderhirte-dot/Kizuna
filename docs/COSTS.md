# Costs

No VoxRally subscription, license gate, artificial resolution cap or streaming duration quota is implemented. Direct LAN/VPN/P2P media does not use a VoxRally media server. Electricity, connectivity and any metered traffic remain the users' costs.

No paid cloud services were provisioned. Electron, React, Vite, TypeScript, ws and Zod are dependencies distributed with their licenses. A public STUN endpoint is used for candidate discovery, without an availability guarantee. Public signaling hosting, domain/TLS operations, TURN bandwidth, future SFU capacity and code-signing certificates may have costs depending on the chosen provider. Nothing was purchased.

The app is self-hostable. Free direct P2P cannot be guaranteed across every NAT/firewall. There is currently no hosted VoxRally signaling service. A user-provided reachable server or pre-existing VPN is necessary for different homes.
